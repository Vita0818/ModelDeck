# ModelDeck support

This is a personal local project. No public issue tracker or support service is configured yet. Record reproducible issues in this project, including the ModelDeck version and client version. Remove credentials and private conversation content from logs before sharing.

Client compatibility and known boundaries are documented in [CLIENT_MODELS.md](docs/CLIENT_MODELS.md). Historical upstream support information is preserved under `docs/upstream/` and applies to the upstream project.
